Principal Project · Bachelor's Thesis (UPF 2025-2026)

Robustness Evaluation of AI-Generated Music Detectors Based on Audio Encoder Features

Author: Arnau Martin Riera · Advisor: Martín Rocamora

Context & Problem Statement

The rise of generative platforms like Suno and Udio enables the creation of high-fidelity songs virtually indistinguishable to the human ear. However, neural codec-based decoders (such as Encodec) leave structural fingerprints: so-called "fakeprints". These artifacts are replicas of upsampling operations and strided deconvolutions within Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs).

While statistical detectors based on linear regressions or shallow models achieve 100% accuracy under laboratory conditions, this research exposes their systemic fragility. Detectors do not learn the abstract concept of "real music" or "artificial music"; instead, they memorize the metric rigidity and exact placement of spectral peaks unique to each specific generation network.

Mathematical Foundation of the Artifact:

In a deconvolution layer with a stride k, the zero-upsampling operation periodizes the spectrum, introducing narrow peaks at equidistant intervals at the following frequencies:

n · fs   for integer bins   n ∈ [0, ⌊k/2⌋]

When chaining multiple layers, the maximum number of resulting spectral peaks in the output grows recursively according to the formula:

⌊Pmax/2⌋ + 1,   where   Pmax = ∏ k(i)

This metric signature is canceled out by temporally averaging the Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) across thousands of frames, effectively exposing the underlying architecture of the source model.

Multiband Degradation Pipeline

As the primary contribution of this thesis, a post-processing pipeline was designed and optimized based on the Overlap-Add (OLA) method combined with phase perturbations via millisecond block micro-shifts.

  • ▸ Multiband Decomposition: The signal is isolated into two distinct bands using a fixed cutoff frequency of 4000 Hz.
  • ▸ Micro-Timing Displacements: Random, asynchronous phase perturbations are applied exclusively to the mid-high band. This disrupts the rigidity of the temporal spectral grid without compromising musical dynamics.

Research Conclusion: Results from a perceptual test with a control group of N = 20 listeners conclusively demonstrate that micro-timing alterations and phase decomposition are completely transparent to the human ear. This opens a critical and urgent debate within the music industry regarding the reliability of current spectral-architecture-based detectors against adversarial attacks.

01 / 02 · Side Project

fixyoursound

What started as a lightweight vibe-coding test evolved into a powerful, full-stack educational ecosystem engineered to democratize music theory and audio engineering. Instead of treating AI as a "black-box" automation system that generates flattened audio files and replaces human creativity, fixyoursound leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) as an interactive, real-time co-producer and technical mentor. The platform bridges the gap between creative abstract intent and concrete technical execution, guiding modern producers through complex sound synthesis, compositional structures, and algorithmic rhythm analysis directly inside the browser.

▸ Sound Replicator

An advanced natural language interface for audio synthesis. Users describe complex acoustic textures, vintage synth patches, or specific guitar tones in plain English (e.g., "a dark, detuned, low-pass techno bass"). The AI acts as an expert engineer, reverse-engineering the text into raw synthesis data—mapping out precise oscillator types, envelope shapes (ADSR), LFO modulation routings, and dynamic FX chains (filters, saturation, delays). Instead of delivering a sample, it renders the sound in real-time using native web audio synthesis and provides an explicit, interactive patching guide that explains the physical science behind how each parameter shapes the frequency spectrum.

▸ Interactive Beat Maker & Chord Architect

A dynamic compositional tool that translates algorithmic text prompts into complete MIDI sequences, functional chord progressions, and intricate drum patterns. The system parses structured data configurations to feed a responsive, interactive step-sequencer and digital piano visualizer. Every generated groove is accompanied by a deep architectural and music theory breakdown, demystifying syncopation, polyrhythms, harmonic tension, and modal choices. This teaches users exactly why a rhythm works and how it creates cultural 'vibe' and movement.

▸ Collaborative Real-Time Community

A secure, full-stack social hub designed to break the isolation of bedroom production. Backed by a relational database model (Prisma + Supabase PostgreSQL) and integrated session handling (NextAuth), it allows emerging producers to log in, share their creative AI prompts, critique structural loop breakdowns, and collaboratively build knowledge in real-time.
